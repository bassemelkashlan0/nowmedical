Same-Day Medical Care for Southwest Calgary Residents
If you are searching for a walk-in clinic in SW Calgary, Now Medical Clinic provides same-day medical care for residents of Evergreen, Shawnessy, Bridlewood, Somerset, Millrise, and surrounding southwest communities.
We are open 7 days a week until 11 PM. No appointment is required.
SW Calgary patients choose walk-in care instead of waiting in emergency departments such as Rockyview General Hospital.

Conditions We Treat
Our walk-in doctors provide care for:
• Cold, flu, cough & sore throat
• Minor injuries and sprains
• Skin infections and allergic reactions
• Prescription renewals
• Chronic condition flare-ups
• Pediatric and adult medical concerns
If your condition is not life-threatening, a walk-in clinic can often provide faster access than emergency care.
