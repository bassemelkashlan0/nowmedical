Same-Day Medical Care for Southeast Calgary Residents
If you are searching for a walk-in clinic in SE Calgary, Now Medical Clinic provides same-day medical care for residents of Mahogany, Auburn Bay, Cranston, Seton, Copperfield, and surrounding southeast communities.
We are open 7 days a week until 11 PM. No appointment is required.
SE Calgary patients choose walk-in care instead of waiting in emergency departments such as South Health Campus.
