Same-Day Medical Care for Downtown Calgary
If you are searching for a walk-in clinic in Downtown Calgary, Now Medical Clinic provides same-day medical care for residents and professionals in Beltline, East Village, Bridgeland, Mission, and surrounding central Calgary communities.
We are open 7 days a week until 11 PM. No appointment is required.
Downtown residents and professionals choose walk-in care instead of waiting in hospital emergency departments.
