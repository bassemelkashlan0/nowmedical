        Same-Day Medical Care for Northwest Calgary Residents
If you are searching for a walk-in clinic in NW Calgary, Now Medical Clinic provides same-day medical care for residents of Tuscany, Crowfoot, Sage Hill, Evanston, Nolan Hill, and surrounding northwest communities.
We are open 7 days a week until 11 PM. No appointment is required.
NW Calgary residents choose walk-in care instead of waiting in hospital emergency departments such as Foothills Medical Centre.
